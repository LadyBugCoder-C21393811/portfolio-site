Accessibility Navigation App (Work in Progress)

This mobile application is designed to help users assess and review the accessibility of locations for people with disabilities. It allows users to search for a location, rate accessibility using a colour-coded system, and leave detailed reviews. The goal is to create a shared community resource that supports informed decision-making for wheelchair users and others with accessibility needs.

I have completed the core rating system, location search, and review structure. Future development will include expanding the database, improving the interface, and adding features that require additional funding.

I am currently looking for testers, especially wheelchair users, to help evaluate accessibility ratings and provide feedback.

Technologies: Java, SQL, MS Access, Eclipse, NetBeans

Screen Distance Safety App (Work in Progress)

This mobile app uses heat and motion sensors to detect when a child is too close to a screen. When triggered, the app asks an age-appropriate question to encourage healthier screen habits. Parents can adjust question difficulty to suit their child’s age and learning level.

The detection logic and parental control system are designed, and early prototypes are functional. Future development will focus on studying camera angles, improving sensor accuracy, and refining the question system.

Technologies: Java, SQL, MS Access, Eclipse, NetBeans
