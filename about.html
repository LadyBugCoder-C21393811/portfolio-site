About Me

I’m Megan Cahill, a Business Computing graduate with professional experience in software development, IT administration, and community-based coding mentorship. I enjoy working at the intersection of technology and problem solving, and I’m passionate about creating projects that make a real impact.

During my software engineering internship at Workday, I contributed to the development and testing of a complex payroll system as part of a Scrum team. I gained hands-on experience with debugging, accuracy testing, and collaborating across technical and business functions.

Before that, I worked as an IT Administrator supporting over 100 users. This role strengthened my skills in troubleshooting, network maintenance, security protocols, hardware checks, and system setup — giving me a strong foundation in IT operations.

I also volunteer as a lead mentor with CoderDojo, teaching coding skills to young people aged 6 to 16. This experience has helped me develop strong communication, planning, and presentation abilities while supporting my local community.

My projects range from accessibility-focused mobile applications to sensor-based educational apps and database systems. I enjoy building solutions that help people, and I’m continuing to grow my skills in software development, web technologies, and IT systems.
